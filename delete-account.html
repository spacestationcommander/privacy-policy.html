Delete Your Space Station Commander Account

If you would like your Space Station Commander account and associated data deleted, you may request deletion at any time.

How to request account deletion

  1. Send an email to spacestationcommander@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line Delete My Account.
  3. Include your Commander name and the email address linked to your account.

What will be deleted

  • Your account
  • Your colony
  • Your fleets
  • Your buildings
  • Your research progress
  • Your battle history
  • Your stored game progress
  • Your notification tokens

What may be retained

Certain server logs or records may be retained for a limited period where required for security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance.

Deletion timeframe

Verified deletion requests are normally completed within 30 days.
