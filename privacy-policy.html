Welcome to Space Station Commander, developed by Omni Orbit Games.

Notifications

If notifications are enabled, Space Station Commander may send notifications including:

  • Fleet arrivals

Notifications can be disabled at any time through your device settings.

  • Improve stability and performance
  • Provide customer support

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

  • Google Play Services

Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information from unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration or destruction.

Although no online service can guarantee absolute security, we continuously work to improve the protection of your information.

Data Retention

Your account information and gameplay data are stored while your account remains active.

If you request deletion of your account, we will permanently remove your personal information and game data unless we are legally required to retain certain information.
